How it works

From raw data to a clean file in three steps.

1

Upload

A FIRE flat file, or a CSV in the IRS template for any of the 37 IRIS forms.

2

Validate

Every record checked against the form rules, with the row, the column and the fix for each problem.

3

Fix and download

We fix formatting errors for you and show every change; you correct the rest in the browser and re-check free. Clean FIRE files become validated filings and downloadable XML.
